[docs.acmesync.example/limits, updated 2026-08-14]
Plans: Free (2 connections, sync every 15 min), Pro $49/mo (10 connections, sync every 60 seconds, 1,000 API requests/hour), Business $199/mo (unlimited connections, sync every 60 seconds, 10,000 API requests/hour, SSO).
Real-time sync (webhooks) is in beta and available on Business only.
Export: CSV only. No bulk export of sync history.
Data is stored in a single US region. EU region on the roadmap, no date.
Records per sync run capped at 50,000.
